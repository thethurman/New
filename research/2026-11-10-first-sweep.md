## 3. Registration plan (six weeks)

Nothing here costs more than local license fees and insurance. Any site charging for SAM.gov registration is a third party.

**Week 1: foundations.** Everything else checks against these.
1. Confirm the company is active with the Georgia Secretary of State (https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov). Write down the exact legal name, address and formation date.
2. Make the IRS record (EIN letter) match the state record character for character. Mismatches are the main cause of SAM.gov delays.
3. Occupational tax certificate (business license). First find out whether the address is inside Snellville city limits or unincorporated Gwinnett; much of 30039 is unincorporated.
4. Enroll in E-Verify (free). Georgia requires a notarized E-Verify affidavit on public service contracts over $2,499.99.
5. Get insurance quotes: general liability, auto, workers' comp, and technology errors and omissions / cyber for the IT lines. Limits are set per bid.
6. Write a one-page capability statement and have a W-9 ready.
7. Book free counseling now, since there are waits: Georgia Tech APEX Accelerator (government contracting), UGA SBDC Gwinnett (678-985-6820), SCORE.

**Week 2: the slow item and the state.**
8. SAM.gov entity registration (free; allow 2 to 6 weeks). This produces the federal ID numbers (UEI and CAGE).
9. Register in GA@WORK Marketplace for Suppliers, which replaced Team Georgia Marketplace on July 1, 2026 (start at https://doas.ga.gov/state-purchasing/getting-started-supplier). The commodity codes you pick drive which bid emails you get.
10. Gwinnett County supplier portal; self-certify as a Local Small Business (new in 2026). Purchasing: 770-822-7821.
11. Gwinnett County Public Schools supplier registry.

**Week 3: metro portals** (none depend on SAM): DeKalb (iSupplier plus OpenGov), Fulton (BidNet Direct, choose the free "Limited Access" tier), City of Atlanta (atlsuppliers.com), Cobb (OpenGov), MARTA, Atlanta Public Schools. Snellville has no portal; ask the City Clerk to add you to the bidder list.

**Week 4: certifications.** Georgia Business Certification (small / minority / woman / veteran owned; free, applied for inside GA@WORK). DeKalb Local Small Business Enterprise certification, which matters because DeKalb primes are expected to subcontract to certified firms. Fulton and Atlanta programs if eligible.

**Week 5: after SAM activates.** Complete the SBA Small Business Search profile. Set SAM.gov saved searches. Register as a subvendor with Computer Aid, Inc., the single prime on Georgia's statewide IT staffing contract (https://www.cai.io/msp/georgia).

**Week 6: pursue.** Attend one "how to do business with" session, bid one small job, prune the commodity codes after two weeks of emails.

### Things that changed in 2026

- SBA's 8(a) program was overhauled (new rule effective Sep 10, 2026). Treat it as a later question for an APEX counselor, not part of this plan.
- The federal DBE program dropped its race and sex presumptions; Georgia DOT may not be taking new applications. Call 404-631-1273 before spending time on it.
- SAM.gov reportedly no longer requires a notarized administrator letter (one news source; unconfirmed).

### Licensing limits on what you can bid

- **Herbicide or pesticide spraying** needs a Georgia Department of Agriculture Pesticide Contractor License and a licensed applicator on staff. Mowing, cleanup and janitorial work need no state license. A recent Gwinnett grounds bid required this license.
- **Digital forensics** counts as private detective work in Georgia and needs a state license. Keep penetration testing and assessments separate from forensic investigation, or subcontract the forensics.

## 4. Making this weekly

What this run proved about automation:

- **GPR is fully automatable.** `scripts/gpr_open_bids.js` pulls every open event in one request. A weekly run can diff against last week's file to show only new and closing-soon bids.
- **SAM.gov** has an official search API; it needs a free key from a SAM.gov account.
- **Events**: the Gwinnett Entrepreneur Center, chamber calendars (Gwinnett, Greater Eastside, NAMAR) and Meetup groups offer calendar feeds. Eventbrite needs a browser. These hosts must be allowed in the environment's network settings first.
- **Local portals not on GPR** (Gwinnett's own solicitations page, DeKalb OpenGov, BidNet): plain pages or browser-only; add after GPR and SAM are running.
